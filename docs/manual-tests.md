# Manual tests

Behavior that the automated tests (`python tools/run_tests.py`) don't cover and that has to be checked by a person. The automated tests cover how the project is put together (it opens on the shooter level with the game's own mode, the template's Blueprints load against our C++ classes, nothing any asset needs is missing), the rules as plain functions (movement numbers, weapon damage and spread, where the killed come back, the scoreboard's order, the kill limit), and, playing the game in the editor (`CheeseStrike.Play.*`), that the character walks, sprints, slides, crouches and jumps at the right speeds, that the weapon fires, aims, empties and reloads by its numbers, that a character carries a primary, the pistol and the knife and changes between them by their keys (each with its own rounds and draw time, a chosen primary carried from the next life, the server following the change, the pistol's and the knife's damage and the stroke in the back, the modes' one-slot and one-weapon calls), every weapon's numbers against the design's table, and that a far shot is heard quieter and duller, and that over the network a kill is scored and shown, the fallen body comes to rest on both machines with every joint within its ragdoll's limits, the chest not twisted round on the hips and the face in place, the killed come back at a start with full health and protection, and the match ends at the kill limit with a winner; the modes and streaks as plain functions (what each mode is, who takes a round, who may hurt whom, where a side comes in, when streaks are earned, where a Cheese Strike's wheels land and what they do), and, played, that a team wins Deathmatch at its kill limit with friendly fire off, that a round of Elimination ends when a side is out and the next brings everyone back, and that the three streaks are called in (from the host and from a joiner) and score for their callers; that a hit reaches the hurt player's machine as a damage arc pointing at the shooter; and the HUD's rules as plain functions (`CheeseStrike.HUD.*`: how the minimap turns, cuts its shapes and picks what to show by height, which way an arc points, how red the edges get, what the row of players and the score summary say, how name tags shrink, how the death screen's cheese runs). How the HUD looks is checked here.

**Keeping this current:** when a change adds or alters behavior that an automated test can't cover, add or update a row here in the same pull request. When a row becomes covered by an automated test, delete it.

**What to run:** the sections for whatever a change touches. Run everything before sharing a build with other people.
