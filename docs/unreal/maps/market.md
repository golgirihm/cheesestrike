# Rind Market

A town square at dusk, about 104 by 74 m at life size, for up to sixteen players. Market stalls of cheese fill the square, houses of cheese two storeys high stand round it, and in its middle a stone clock tower, never eaten, is the landmark and the high ground. It is built by `tools/art/build_market.py` into `Content/CheeseStrike/Maps/Lvl_RindMarket`, from the blocks in `tools/art/map_kit.py` ([principles.md](principles.md) says how maps are made). The mice eat the stalls first, then the houses' fronts and walls, so the crowded market becomes one open square watched from the tower, and the houses become timber frames under their roofs that you can see through.

## The art

The ground is cobbles: setts laid in fans in the square, square setts in the streets, stone flags under the arcade. The clock tower, the arcade's piers, the well house and the wash house are sandstone; the town walls at each end are rubble stone; the houses' frames, floors, stairs and doors are timber and planks; their roofs and the outer houses' are clay tiles. The outer houses round the edge are plaster fronts in quiet colours (cream, grey, rose, sage, blue, white), with doors, windows (some lit) and shutters. These are Poly Haven textures (CC0), downloaded by `tools/art/polyhaven.py` and imported by `tools/art/import_polyhaven.py`, which also makes their materials on the maps' `M_Surface`, drawn from world space. The stalls' awnings are striped canvas (`M_Awning`, the stripes laid across the world, the linen's weave over them). The lamp posts, wall lanterns, barrels, crates, baskets and benches are Poly Haven models; the lamps' glass glows (`M_PropGlow` lights the near-black of their own textures), and so do the clock's four faces and the lit windows (`M_Glow`). The cheese houses' roofs are tiles on their corner posts, with plastered gables; they stay when the walls are eaten.

It is lit as a town's evening (`dusk()` over the kit's dusk preset): a low sun from the south-west across the lanes, a clear sky, little haze, the picture a little darker than the kit's, and the lamps on: lamp posts round the tower and the square's corners, in the yards and courts, lanterns by every gate, on the tower, along High Street and the alleys, lamps under the arcade and in every house and covered way.

## Layout

West is The Gouda Guys' side, east the Mozza Radicals'; the map is mirrored across the middle (x to -x), so neither side has a better half. Each side comes in at its **gate**, a yard behind the old town wall; from it three ways lead into the town: **High Street** along the north, **Main Street** into the square, and **the alleys** along the south.

Free-for-all has fourteen more starts (tagged "Free"), seven a side: High Street, the yard, the court, the alleys, the square, under the arcade and the lane. Beyond the outer houses, for the view from the tower and nothing else: more roofs, and a church's spire to the north-east.

### How it plays

- **Three lanes.** High Street is the long lane: medium lines either side of the middle, broken by the well house, the outer stairs and the sheds, so that no line runs along it from gate to gate (the longest is about 48 m). The square is the middle: close fighting among the stalls at first, round the tower. The alleys are close and twisting: the wash house, the woodsheds, the drying rack and the covered way.
- **Connectors.** Through the houses: the arcade houses join High Street, the yard and the arcade (and upstairs, the gallery); the balcony houses join the square, the alleys and the court; the Inns and Bakeries join the square, the lane and the yards and courts. The Clock Passage and the Snicket join the lanes to the square at the middle, each a choke with a turn at its end (the well house in front of the passage's north end; the tower's pier in the line between them).
- **High ground.** The gallery over the arcade, the balconies, the houses' upper windows, the bridges, and the belfry. The belfry has one way up, a flight of stairs that ends in the open floor of the loggia, and from up there you are seen from every house round the square, the gallery and the balconies; its parapet is crouch cover only. Early, the stalls' awnings hide whoever is under them from it.
- **Timings.** From a gate start at a sprint: the square's edge down Main Street in about 5 s, the tower in about 7.5 s, the well house along High Street in about 8 s, the wash house through the alleys in about 9 s.
- **The starts.** Behind the town wall, and inside each gate a wall that hides the yard; no line from the lanes in front of the gates, nor from the belfry, reaches a start (`CheeseStrike.Play.RindMarketHoldsTogether` checks it).

The mice come in at ten points (`MouseEntry`, in the `Mice` folder): over the rooftops into the square (off the balcony houses, the Inns and the Bakeries) and over the outer houses into High Street and the alleys. Eight come, at eight of the ten. Each lands 5 m in, on open ground: what is raised above head height and walked on by nobody (the awnings, the roofs, the carts' loads) is kept off the navigation mesh, so that no mouse lands on top of it.

## The cheese, and what the mice change

There are 1,092 blocks of cheese, all of them edible; the full ten mice come. They keep to the mice's pace (`tools/play/market_mice.txt`), so the square is bare about four and a half minutes after they come: the stalls, the stores, the courts and the alleys' cheese in the first four minutes, then the arcade and balcony houses' walls, and the Inns and Bakeries last. What each structure does before the mice, and what eating it does:

What never goes: the clock tower, the town walls and their gates, the well house, the wash house, the woodsheds and High Street's sheds, the arcade's piers, the gallery's floor and the Clock Arch, the houses' frames, floors, stairs and roofs, the outer stairs, the balconies' floors, the stalls' frames and awnings, and the bridges' beams. So High Street and the alleys never have a line from gate to gate, Main Street's line ends at the tower, and nobody ever starts over a hole.

## King of the Hill

King of the Hill's hill goes round four spots (`hills()`, `ACheeseStrikeHillSpot`), a minute at each: **the square north of the tower**, by its north door, **Gouda's High Street** west of the well house, **the square south of the tower**, and **Mozza's High Street**. The square's two are on the middle line and the High Street's are twins; all are on cobbles, which the mice never eat.

## Tests and tooling

`CheeseStrike.Play.EveryMapHasFairHills` checks its hill spots: three to five, each on a floor the mice never eat, clear and level round its middle, on the navigation mesh and reached from both sides' starts, well away from them, the first as far from both and the round favouring neither. `CheeseStrike.Play.RindMarketHoldsTogether` plays the map: starts (eight a side and the free ones), every structure there with its blocks whole and none in the tower, its size, the lines that stone and timber close for good (traced past the cheese, as after the mice: High Street and the alleys from end to end, Main Street at the tower, the tower's pier between its doors, every start hidden from the lanes in front of the gates and from the belfry), the houses' walls, windows, the gallery's parapet and a stall's counter before the mice, a way on the navigation mesh from a gate to every floor (the tower's landing and belfry, the gallery, the houses' upper floors, both bridges, the balcony, the store, the covered way), and a walk up both of the tower's flights, a house's stairs, the outer stairs, out onto the gallery, over both bridges and through the Clock Passage. `CheeseStrike.Play.AfterTheMice.RindMarket` eats all its cheese and checks every start has ground and a way to every other. `tools/play/market_tour.txt` takes pictures of it from set places, and `tools/play/market_mice.txt` pictures over the square every minute as the mice eat it with bots fighting, with the cheese left in the log (`tools/play.ps1 -Map /Game/CheeseStrike/Maps/Lvl_RindMarket -StartAt -4850,0,100,0`; from Git Bash, set `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1`). Its lobby picture is `python tools/art/map_picture.py /Game/CheeseStrike/Maps/Lvl_RindMarket market.png -6400 -5000 3000 -24 40 --start -4850,0,100,0`.

The map has about 2,700 actors, over the principles' 2,500: 1,092 blocks of cheese and about 1,470 static blocks and props (the town's houses, stairs and fronts). Changing the map is a change to `tools/art/build_market.py`, then running it (it rebuilds the level from nothing) and the tests; its surfaces and props are `tools/art/polyhaven.py` (downloads) and `tools/art/import_polyhaven.py` (imports and materials), run once before it.
