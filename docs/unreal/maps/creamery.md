# The Creamery

The first real map: a cheese works, about 130 by 100 m at life size (390 by 300 Godot metres, between Curd City and the small arenas), for up to sixteen players. A sprint from one dock to the other takes about 20 seconds. It is built by `tools/art/build_creamery.py` into `Content/CheeseStrike/Maps/Lvl_Creamery`, from the blocks in `tools/art/map_kit.py` ([principles.md](principles.md) says how maps are made). Most of what stands in it is cheese: two houses, two tunnels, the hall's racks, catwalks and the edges of its decks, a wall of wheels across the yard, walls in front of the docks, pallets, stacks and a bridge. The mice turn it from a closed works of houses, tunnels and racks into an open one of frames and long lines.

## The art

Its walls and floors are blocks (the engine's cube and cylinder) wearing Megascans surfaces from Fab: concrete floors, an asphalt yard, shuttered concrete for the outer wall, corrugated sheets for the hall and the cold stores, painted metal for the racks, rails, pipes, frames and silos, and roller doors on the docks and the doors that open. `M_Surface` (`tools/art/build_materials.py`) draws a surface from world space on all three axes, so a block of any size shows it at its real scale, varies it over large areas so its repeat doesn't show, and repaints it in the map's colours. Its things are Fab models: shipping containers (Sousinho Games', repainted, with the shipping line's lettering painted over), Megascans tarped crates on pallets, jersey barriers, barrels, drums, plastic crates, a workbench and a cabinet in each house and under the mezzanine, fluorescent lamps in the hall; with oil stains, cracks in the asphalt, rust leaking down the wall and a number on each dock door as decals. `tools/art/import_fab.py` imports them from the downloads (it names each file and where on Fab it came from; `Content/LICENSES.md` has the listings). The catwalks' beams are cheese in red wax (`M_CheeseWax`, `tools/art/wax_material.py`): cheese the mice never eat.

A model standing for cover is scaled to fill the block the map's layout gives it (a crate on its pallet fills its block exactly, the Squeeze is two stacks of crates filling its two blocks), so the layout plays as below, and every model's collision, for movement and for shots and sight lines, is its box: cover is as big as it looks, and there are no slits between stacked crates. Clutter stands against walls and in corners, off the lanes, the ramps and the lines the tests check.

## Layout

West is The Gouda Guys' side, east the Mozza Radicals'; the map is mirrored across the middle (x to -x), so neither side has a better half. From west to east: a **dock**, a **plaza** with its **Curing House**, then three lanes side by side, then the other plaza, house and dock.

| Place | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| The Docks | each end | a loading platform 1.2 m up along the outer wall, with two ramps down, roller doors behind; eight starts each, four up, four down, facing the middle, a side's starts (tagged with it) |
| The Plazas | between the docks and the lanes | open ground with pallets of cheese wheels and a load waiting by the hall's corner |
| The Curing Houses | on each plaza, south of the line between the dock and the hall's main door | two storeys of cheese, 12 by 14 m, on a steel frame: a door at each end (towards the dock and towards the tunnel's mouth) and one onto the plaza, windows onto the plaza on both floors, and upstairs onto the hall's main door, the dock and the canal; stairs inside along the south wall. The high ground over the plaza |
| The Loading Yard | the north lane | shipping containers alone and stacked, a trailer, barriers, lamps, stacks of cheese between the containers; cut in two at the middle by the **Wheel Stack** |
| The Squeeze | the walkway between the yard and the hall | two overlapping stacks of crates by the hall's north wall: the only way past the Wheel Stack, zig-zagging, with no line along it |
| The Aging Hall | the middle lane | a hall 48 by 36 m, 9 m high, skylights in its roof; **racks** of cheese wheels in rows either side of a middle aisle, the **press** in the middle; the **mezzanine** 4 m up along the south wall and the **gallery** along the north, each with ramps at either end and cheese along its edge to crouch behind; two **catwalks** across the hall between them, over the racks; main doors at each end (8 m), two doors each in the north wall (4 m, sealed) and the south wall (4 m, doors that open, from the tunnels) |
| The Rind Tunnels | along the hall's south wall, each side | a covered way of cheese 20 m long, from the walkway's middle to the plaza, its roof hung from the hall's wall; a door into the hall and a way out onto the canal's steps |
| The Whey Canal | the south lane | a channel 8 m wide and 3 m deep, ramps down at each end, steps out along its north wall, two concrete bridges and the **Cheese Bridge** in the middle |
| Tank Row | south of the canal | steel silos, barriers, cheese waiting by the silos, and a pipe rack along its length; pipe bridges over the canal and the tunnels to the hall |

Its places are named (`ACheeseStrikePlace`): Gouda Dock and Mozza Dock, the plazas, the Curing Houses, tunnels, yards and catwalks by side, and The Wheel Stack, The Squeeze, The Hall, The Press, The Mezzanine, The Gallery, The Walkway, The Canal, The Cheese Bridge and Tank Row.

Free-for-all has fourteen more starts (tagged "Free"), seven a side through the lanes: the plaza, the yard twice, the hall north and south of its middle aisle, the walkway past the tunnel's mouth, and Tank Row. In free-for-all everyone is an enemy and comes back at the start furthest from them all, so without these a match would begin and come back in a crowd on the docks; with them the bots spread through every lane (`CheeseStrike.Play.BotsPlayTheCreamery`).

The mice come over the outer wall at ten points (`MouseEntry`, in the level's `Mice` folder), mirrored like the map: four along the north wall over the yard (not where the Wheel Stack meets it), four along the south over Tank Row, and one at each end south of the docks. Ten come, one at each. They take the dock walls, the plazas' pallets, the houses and the cheese by the silos first, the nearest cheese low down, then the tunnels, the Cheese Bridge, the Wheel Stack, the hall's seals and edges, and last the racks.

The bots walk a navigation mesh built when the level starts, in the bounds of its `Navigation` volume, and rebuilt where it changes, as it does when the mice eat cheese; their paths are found afresh as it changes. The doors don't block it: a bot walks up to a door and it opens.

Beyond the outer wall, for a skyline and nothing else: two cold stores, a chimney, a battery of tall silos, offices.

## The cheese, and what the mice change

Every block of cheese is an `ACheeseStrikeCheese` (`Source/CheeseStrike/Map/`) with the design's 400 of eating in it and the name of the structure it belongs to. There are 1,026 the mice can eat, and four they can't (the catwalks' waxed beams); the design's one mouse to sixty blocks puts the full ten mice on it. They eat at a pace that leaves the map bare about four and a half minutes after they come (CheeseStrikeMice::Pace): the outside goes first, the middle last. What each structure does before the mice, and what eating it does:

| Structure | Blocks | Before | Eaten |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CuringHouseGouda**, **CuringHouseMozza** | 97 each | A house on each plaza: the plaza's high ground, its upstairs windows on the plaza, the hall's main door and the canal; a way through from the dock to the tunnel. | Its walls come down from the ground, column by column, and the roof onto the upper floor: what is left is an open deck on posts with its stairs. The plaza's best spot becomes its most exposed. |
| **RindTunnelGouda**, **RindTunnelMozza** | 23 each | A dark covered way along the hall's south side, the only way to the hall's south door: close fighting, unseen from Tank Row and the canal. | The walkway is open again: the hall's south door is reached from the canal's steps and Tank Row, and the south lane and the hall join. |
| **WheelStack** | 100 | A wall of wheels four high across the yard. The north lane has no long line; it is fought close, through the Squeeze. | The yard opens from dock to dock: a 130 m line for whoever holds a dock, and a second wide way past the hall. The lane turns from close fighting to long. |
| **HallSeal** | 24 | The hall's two north doors are stopped. The hall is entered from the ends and the south. | The hall opens onto the yard: two more ways in, and the yard and the hall become one fight. |
| **Racks** | 360 | Shelves of wheels, a wall each, making aisles across the hall; the hall is close and full of corners. | Bare steel frames you can see and shoot through: the hall becomes one open floor, watched from the mezzanine, the gallery and the catwalks. |
| **Catwalks** | 26 | Two bridges of cheese over the racks at 4 m, from the mezzanine to the gallery: a way across the hall above the fight. | Two waxed beams each, which the mice never eat: a balance walk for a careful player, too narrow for the bots. The hall's upper level splits in two. |
| **HallEdges** | 22 | Cheese a metre high along the mezzanine's and the gallery's edges, to crouch behind. | Bare rails: whoever is up there is seen from the whole floor. |
| **CheeseBridge** | 32 | The middle crossing over the canal. | Its steel girders: the canal is crossed at the two concrete bridges, by going down into it, or along a girder; the south lane splits in two. |
| **DockWallGouda**, **DockWallMozza** | 48 each | Low walls (2 m) in front of each dock, covering the ways out. | A side's way out is open to the plaza: holding the plaza starts to mean pinning a side in. |
| **PalletsGouda**, **PalletsMozza** | 40 each | Wheels on pallets, crouching cover across the plazas. | The plazas are bare ground to be crossed fast. |
| **YardCheeseGouda**, **YardCheeseMozza** | 15 each | Stacks of wheels and blocks between the containers. | The containers are the yard's only cover. |
| **TankCheeseGouda**, **TankCheeseMozza** | 7 each | A cube of blocks and a stack of wheels by the silos. | Tank Row is silos and barriers. |

So the map starts closed (three separate lanes, a house and a tunnel on each side, short lines, cover everywhere) and opens as a match goes on: the plazas lose their houses and pallets, the tunnels open the walkway, the lanes join through the hall's north doors and the yard, the long lines appear, the hall loses its cover and its catwalks, and the south loses its middle crossing. What anyone stands on is never eaten from under them except the Cheese Bridge's and the catwalks' decks, which leave beams.

## Tests and tooling

`CheeseStrike.Play.TheCreameryHoldsTogether` plays the map: starts for sixteen, every structure there with its blocks whole, its size, the lines above (the Wheel Stack closes the yard, the Squeeze breaks the walkway, the Seals close the doors, the press stops the hall's door-to-door line, the racks are full and the aisles clear, the house hides its ground floor and looks out upstairs, the tunnel hides from the canal and from above, the catwalks and the gallery's edge are cheese), and a walk up or across every ramp, the house's stairs, the tunnel and its door into the hall, a catwalk, the steps and the Cheese Bridge. `CheeseStrike.Play.AfterTheMice.TheCreamery` eats all its cheese and checks every start has ground and a way to every other; `CheeseStrike.Play.AHouseComesDownFromItsFoot` eats the Gouda Curing House from the ground and checks nothing is left standing on nothing; `CheeseStrike.Play.ADoorOpensForWhoeverComes` walks through the west tunnel's door into the hall. `CheeseStrike.Play.TheHostPicksTheMap` picks it in the lobby and checks everyone arrives. `tools/play/creamery_tour.txt` takes pictures of it from set places, and `tools/play/creamery_mice.txt` pictures from above every minute as the mice eat it, with bots fighting (`tools/play.ps1 -Map /Game/CheeseStrike/Maps/Lvl_Creamery`; from Git Bash, set `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1` so the path isn't rewritten). Its lobby picture is `python tools/art/map_picture.py /Game/CheeseStrike/Maps/Lvl_Creamery creamery.png -7400 -5600 3600 -28 36`.

Changing the map is a change to `tools/art/build_creamery.py`, then running it (it rebuilds the level from nothing) and the tests.
