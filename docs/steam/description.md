# CheeseStrike: Gouda Overload, the Steam page

Text for the store page, to paste into Steamworks (Store Page Admin). Keep it to what the game does: when a feature changes, change this page with it.

## Short description

A first-person shooter where all the cover is cheese, and three minutes in, giant vermin arrive to eat it. Fight in teams or alone, up to 16 players with bots, and watch the map open up around you.

## About this game

Most of what you hide behind in CheeseStrike is cheese: walls, crates, crouch-high stacks, whole buildings. Three minutes into a match the vermin come: giant rats that pour in over the walls and start eating, block by block, until the map is bare. The corridor you held becomes an open floor, the sniper's nest loses its walls, and every fight gets closer and faster than the last.

Play in six modes: Free-for-all, and Deathmatch in two teams; Elimination, one life a round; Execution, where a player hurt enough goes down with only a pistol and must be finished up close with a knife or a stomp, or revived by a teammate before an enemy gets there; Gun Game, a ladder of weapons that ends with the knife; and King of the Hill, a scoring zone that moves round the map every minute.

Kills in a row earn streaks: the Unmanned Aerial Cheese shows your side where every enemy is, the Cheese Strike drops wheels of gouda on a spot you pick from the map, and the Gouda Overload is the one everyone hears coming.

Matches hold up to 16 players, eight a side, with bots of three skills to fill any empty seat, so a match is never short. Host a lobby, set the rules, and invite your Steam friends from the friends panel, or join the games they are in. Play with mouse and keyboard or a gamepad.

The arenas are a creamery, a labyrinth, a grotto, a covered market, a fondue factory and a kitchen, each built for its cheese to be eaten.

## Features

- All the cover is cheese, and giant vermin eat it during the match: every map changes from tight to open as you play.
- Six modes: Free-for-all, Deathmatch, Elimination, Execution, Gun Game and King of the Hill.
- Execution: go down instead of dying, hold out with your pistol, and be revived or finished with a knife or a stomp.
- Three kill streaks: a scouting drone, an airstrike of cheese wheels and the Gouda Overload.
- Up to 16 players in a match, with bots in three skills to fill the seats.
- Steam lobbies and a friends panel to invite friends and join their games.
- Six arenas, each built around cheese that disappears.
- Full gamepad support, with the controls shown for the device in your hands.

## System requirements (estimated)

These are a guess from the engine's needs and the development PC, not yet measured on a range of machines.

### Minimum

- OS: Windows 10 64-bit
- Processor: 4-core, 3 GHz (Intel Core i5-8400 or AMD Ryzen 5 2600)
- Memory: 8 GB RAM
- Graphics: DirectX 12 card with 4 GB (NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 or AMD Radeon RX 580)
- DirectX: Version 12
- Network: Broadband Internet connection
- Storage: 6 GB available space

### Recommended

- OS: Windows 11 64-bit
- Processor: 6-core, 3.5 GHz (Intel Core i5-12400 or AMD Ryzen 5 5600)
- Memory: 16 GB RAM
- Graphics: DirectX 12 card with 8 GB (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 or AMD Radeon RX 6650 XT)
- DirectX: Version 12
- Network: Broadband Internet connection
- Storage: 6 GB available space, SSD
