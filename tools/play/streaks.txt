# The streaks in a team match with bots, for looking at what they show: the
# host calls in an Unmanned Aerial Cheese (its sweeps mark the enemy) and
# looks up at the drone; then an enemy bot is given the Gouda Overload,
# which it calls in on the host's side (the siren, the warning, the rain).
# Run with tools/play.ps1 -Map /Game/CheeseStrike/Maps/Lvl_Creamery -Bots
# -StartAt none -Extra "?Mode=Deathmatch?Bots=5".
wait 4
shot streaks_1_start
cmd CheeseStrikeGiveStreak 1
wait 0.3
shot streaks_2_ready
tap Streak
wait 2.2
shot streaks_3_drone_marks
look 0 45
wait 0.2
shot streaks_4_drone_sky
look 0 -45
wait 1
cmd CheeseStrikeGiveStreak 3 enemy
wait 2
shot streaks_5_overload_warning
wait 1.5
shot streaks_6_overload_warning
wait 2
shot streaks_7_rain
wait 1.5
shot streaks_8_rain
hold Scoreboard
wait 0.3
shot streaks_9_scoreboard
release Scoreboard
wait 4
shot streaks_10_after
quit
