# The friends panel in the lobby, for looking at it: opened as a click on its
# tile opens it (CheeseStrikeFriends 1) with made-up friends in every state
# (CheeseStrikeSampleFriends), shut as a click on FRIENDS shuts it (0) to its
# tile, and open again with nobody in it. Run with
# tools/play.ps1 -Script tools/play/friends_lobby.txt -Lobby -Extra "?listen" -Res 1600x900 -Tag friends_lobby
wait 6
cmd CheeseStrikeSampleFriends 1
cmd CheeseStrikeFriends 1
wait 1.5
shot friends_lobby_open
cmd CheeseStrikeFriends 0
wait 1
shot friends_lobby_tab
cmd CheeseStrikeSampleFriends 0
cmd CheeseStrikeFriends 1
wait 1
shot friends_lobby_empty
quit
