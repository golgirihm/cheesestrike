# Switching heroes on the front end: a screenshot on the first frame after
# each switch and one thirty frames later, which tools/art/figure_sharpness.py
# compares (both equally sharp: the heroes' textures are kept resident), and
# nothing of the old or new body or the weapon out of place on any frame
# (CheeseStrike.Play.TheFigureChangesHeroWithNothingOutOfPlace checks that
# every frame). Run with tools/play.ps1 -Lobby -FixedFps 30 (so that a wait
# of 0.034 is one frame). The figure is held still, its animation paused
# (CheeseStrike.FigureTurn 0), and the anti-aliasing and Lumen's temporal
# filters turned off (their history settles on a newly shown mesh over its
# first frames), so that the two screenshots of a pair differ only in whether
# the hero's textures and levels of detail are there.
wait 4
cmd CheeseStrike.FigureTurn 0
cmd r.AntiAliasingMethod 0
cmd r.Lumen.ScreenProbeGather.Temporal 0
cmd r.Lumen.Reflections.Temporal 0
wait 0.5
shot switch_0_belica
cmd CheeseStrikeHero Murdock
wait 0.034
shot switch_1_murdock_first
wait 1
shot switch_1_murdock_30
cmd CheeseStrikeHero Wraith
wait 0.034
shot switch_2_wraith_first
wait 1
shot switch_2_wraith_30
cmd CheeseStrikeHero Twinblast
wait 0.034
shot switch_3_twinblast_first
wait 1
shot switch_3_twinblast_30
cmd CheeseStrikeHero Belica
wait 0.034
shot switch_4_belica_first
wait 1
shot switch_4_belica_30
quit
