# A door hung in cheese goes when the cheese round it is eaten, though its
# building stands: the Curing House's north door on The Creamery (the Gouda
# side), seen from the plaza before and after the console eats only the blocks
# within 4 m of the door (CheeseStrikeEatCheese 1 <structure> <x> <y> <z>
# <radius>; CheeseStrikeHung lists what hangs and the blocks round each), then
# the house from further off with its door gone and its walls standing. Run
# with -Map /Game/CheeseStrike/Maps/Lvl_Creamery -StartAt none.
wait 20
cmd CheeseStrikeViewFrom -4000 150 190 -8 -90
wait 2
shot hung_door_1_before
cmd CheeseStrikeHung
cmd CheeseStrikeEatCheese 1 CuringHouseGouda -4000 -625 110 400
wait 2.5
cmd CheeseStrikeHung
shot hung_door_2_after

wait 1
shot hung_door_3_house_stands
