# Choosing where a Cheese Strike falls on the strike map: given the streak,
# the Streak key opens it, the mouse moves the mark to the left,
# the stick moves it back, a shot calls it in, and the wheels land in the
# area. Run with -StartAt 1150,-1450,100,117 (open ground on the shooter
# level), at 1920 by 1080 and 2560 by 1440 (the r.SetRes lines).
cmd r.SetRes 1920x1080w
wait 4
cmd CheeseStrikeGiveStreak 2
wait 0.5
tap Streak
wait 0.3
shot strike_map_1080_open
hold MouseLook -1 0.4
wait 0.4
release MouseLook
wait 0.2
shot strike_map_1080_moved
device gamepad
hold Look 0 1
wait 0.15
release Look
wait 0.2
shot strike_map_1080_pad
cmd r.SetRes 2560x1440w
wait 1.2
shot strike_map_1440
device mouse
tap Fire
wait 0.3
look 0 -12
wait 1.4
shot strike_map_landing
wait 1.0
shot strike_map_after
quit
