# The damage arcs and the red edges, held still for judging them
# (CheeseStrikeHitFrom, CheeseStrike.HUD.HoldArcs and CheeseStrike.HUD.Health):
# one hit from the right, then three from round about, then the edges as health
# falls and pulses. Run alone with -Res 1920x1080. The arcs from real shots over
# the network are CheeseStrike.Play.TheHUDShowsWhereHitsComeFrom's.
wait 4
cmd CheeseStrike.HUD.HoldArcs 1
cmd CheeseStrikeHitFrom 70
wait 0.6
shot hits_1_one_from_the_right
cmd CheeseStrikeHitFrom -135
cmd CheeseStrikeHitFrom 5

shot hits_2_three_round_about

shot edges_2_health_35

wait 0.3

wait 0.4
shot edges_4_health_15_later
