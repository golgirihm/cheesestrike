# The loadout page a count-in puts up: the match starts at once with the
# loadout's eight-second count (tools/play.ps1 -Countdown), the page comes up
# of itself over the match saying the seconds, a pick is taken up at once
# while held, OK puts it away, and the HUD's glyphs come for the last three
# seconds. Run with tools/play.ps1 -Countdown -Res 1280x720.
wait 1.5
shot countin_1_page
cmd CheeseStrikeLoadout munster smoke murdock
wait 1.5
shot countin_2_picked
key Enter
wait 0.5
shot countin_3_dismissed
wait 3.5
shot countin_4_glyphs
wait 2
state
shot countin_5_playing
quit
